🗺️ Founder Roadmap

Everything you need to do

A phased 0–24 month plan plus checklists across the five workstreams — technical, legal/regulatory, business/funding, IP, and team/manufacturing. Built for a part-time start that ramps as your other projects allow.

How to use this: you said launch is 6 months to ~2 years out and you're building this alongside other work. So this roadmap front-loads the cheap, high-leverage steps (learning, feasibility math, IP, one prototype) and defers the expensive, slow steps (certification, manufacturing, hiring) until the thesis is proven. Do the Phase 0 items even on limited time — they de-risk everything else.

The phased plan

Phase 0 · Months 0–3 · Foundation (low cost, high leverage)

Prove the thesis on paper

  • Finish the feasibility math: pick a target mission, size the hybrid powertrain, estimate endurance/payload vs a benchmark electric drone (use the sizing rules).
  • Define the beachhead: one mission + one buyer type (recommend agri-spray endurance).
  • Talk to 10–15 real operators (agri drone service providers, FPOs, survey firms) — validate the endurance pain and willingness to pay.
  • Decide architecture: series-hybrid vs VTOL vs direct-drive (see Technology).
  • Draft a one-page concept + provisional patent idea list (file cheaply, early).
  • Register Startup India / DPIIT; reserve a company name & domain.
Phase 1 · Months 3–9 · Proof of concept

Build the range-extender bench prototype

  • Procure an off-the-shelf engine (DA/3W/HFE class) + generator; build a bench genset.
  • Instrument it: measure BSFC, power output, vibration, thermal load — your CFD/combustion edge starts here.
  • Integrate onto a modified commercial multirotor as a range-extender (buy the airframe, don't build it yet).
  • Demonstrate a tethered/short flight with the hybrid supplying power — first real data point.
  • Capture a verified endurance benchmark vs the stock electric version — this is your pitch centrepiece.
  • Apply to an incubator / iDEX / grant using the data.
Phase 2 · Months 9–18 · Integrated prototype & funding

A flying, mission-representative aircraft

  • Design an integrated airframe around the powertrain (or partner with an airframe maker).
  • Field trials with 2–3 design-partner operators on real jobs.
  • Begin the DGCA type-certification pathway — engage an accredited lab early to understand test scope.
  • Raise a seed round or secure grant/defence-pilot funding on the trial data.
  • File full patents on the powertrain module & control logic.
  • Stand up a small core team (see Team).
Phase 3 · Months 18–24+ · Certify, manufacture, launch

Get to first revenue

  • Complete DGCA Type Certificate for the launch model.
  • Set up small-batch manufacturing / assembly (design for Make-in-India & PLI eligibility).
  • Launch via subsidy channels (Namo Drone Didi / agri subsidies) and DaaS operators.
  • Sell the range-extender module to other OEMs as a second revenue line.
  • Set up service/spares network — engines need maintenance; make it a strength, not a liability.

Technical checklist

  • Powertrain: engine selection → genset design → EFI/control → buffer-battery sizing → vibration isolation → thermal/cooling → energy-management firmware.
  • Airframe: configuration choice → structural design → propulsion integration → weight budget → redundancy/failsafe.
  • Avionics: flight controller (PX4/ArduPilot to start) → GNSS/RTK → sensors → comms/telemetry → optional BVLOS datalink.
  • Payload interface: spray system / sensor mount / cargo bay + power & data taps.
  • Test & validation: bench dyno → tethered flight → free flight → endurance/payload benchmark → field trials → reliability/MTBF.
  • Safety: engine-out behaviour, parachute/failsafe, fuel-system safety, EMI from engine ignition.

Legal & regulatory checklist (India)

  • Company: incorporate (Pvt Ltd typical for fundraising) → GST → bank → DPIIT/Startup India recognition.
  • DGCA / Digital Sky: understand your weight category → plan Type Certification via accredited lab → UIN registration → manufacturer authorisation.
  • Pilots & ops: Remote Pilot Certificates via approved RPTO; airspace-zone compliance for all testing.
  • Testing permissions: secure a legal test site & any flight permissions before flying prototypes.
  • Imports: check rules for importing engines/components (drone imports are restricted; components differ).
  • Standards: track BIS/quality standards, noise & emissions norms (relevant for IC engines), and any defence-specific requirements.
  • Insurance & liability: product & operations liability, especially for spray over crops/people.
Get expert help here. Certification scope, timeline and cost are the biggest non-technical risks. Engage a DGCA-accredited test lab and an aviation-regulatory consultant during Phase 2 — before you freeze the design — so you build to the test, not around it. Confirm every requirement against official DGCA / Digital Sky sources.

Business & funding checklist

  • Funding ladder: personal/friends → grants (ANRF/SERB, DST, MNRE-adjacent, TDB) & incubator → iDEX (defence) → angel/seed → Series A.
  • Non-dilutive first: maximise grants and scheme money before selling equity — hardware R&D is grant-friendly in India.
  • Unit economics: model cost/flight-hour and acres/day vs electric — your whole pitch is operator ROI.
  • Business model: sell drones + sell/licence the powertrain module + optional DaaS/AMC service revenue.
  • Pricing: anchor to subsidy caps (e.g. Namo Drone Didi ₹8 lakh cap) and operator payback period.
  • Financial plan: 24-month burn, certification & tooling capex, hiring plan tied to milestones.

IP & knowledge checklist

  • File early & cheap: provisional patents on the engine optimisation, genset integration, vibration/thermal solution, and hybrid energy-management control.
  • Trade secrets: keep EFI calibration & BSFC maps as protected know-how; NDAs with partners/suppliers.
  • Publish strategically: your academic side can publish the science (builds credibility & recruiting) while patenting the product.
  • Brand: trademark the name/logo once chosen (current "VAYUYAAN" is a placeholder).
  • Institutional IP: if using IIT/university resources, clarify IP ownership & licensing up front.

Team & manufacturing

First hires (in order)

  • Flight-controls / avionics engineer (the skill you don't have)
  • Airframe/mechanical designer
  • Embedded/firmware engineer (energy management)
  • DGCA-certified test pilot / ops lead
  • Regulatory/quality consultant (part-time)

Manufacturing path

  • Start as integrator: buy engine/airframe/avionics, own the powertrain integration
  • Contract-manufacture non-core parts
  • Insource the engine/genset as it becomes your IP & volumes justify tooling
  • Design for PLI & Make-in-India local value-add from the start
  • Build a spares/service network — turn "engines need maintenance" into recurring revenue
