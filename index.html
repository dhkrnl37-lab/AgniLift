01 — The problem

A battery drone's limit isn't its motors. It's its chemistry.

Lithium packs store a fraction of the usable energy that liquid fuel does, for the same weight. Fly longer and you carry more battery; carry more battery and you lift less payload. For long-endurance and heavy-lift work, that trade-off becomes a wall.

02 — The approach

We engineer the powertrain, not just the airframe.

Our primary direction is a series hybrid: a compact engine drives a generator that feeds a buffer battery and the electric motors — keeping precise electric control while carrying far more mission energy than a battery alone.

03 — Architectures under study

Three ways to carry more energy aloft.

Each suits different missions. We are researching where each is genuinely the right answer — not declaring a winner.

Direct drive

Fuel-powered

A compact engine drives the propeller directly. Simplest and most efficient in cruise — suited to fixed-wing endurance, survey and mapping.

Primary Series hybrid

Engine → motors

Engine feeds a generator and buffer battery; electric motors fly the aircraft. Multirotor and VTOL endurance with electric-grade control.

Parallel hybrid

Engine + motor

Both contribute across phases of flight. Peak power on demand for heavy-payload and specialised VTOL research.

The India advantage

Fuel reaches every village.
Charging doesn't.

Rural and remote operations rarely have dependable charging. Liquid fuel already moves through that infrastructure — which is exactly where longer-endurance UAVs are needed most.

05 — Development path

From research to reliable flight.

Each stage is stated as intended work. We do not present later stages as complete.

01 — Feasibility

Define & compare

Mission definition, propulsion comparison, aircraft sizing and safety analysis.

02 — Powerplant

Bench evaluation

Engine selection, propeller matching, vibration analysis and thermal evaluation.

System build

Airframe, propulsion, avionics, controls and payload-interface development.

04 — Flight test

Fly & refine

Progressive test flights, data collection, refinement and reliability assessment.

Field trials

Controlled trials with research, agricultural and industrial collaborators.

06 — How we work

We publish what we can prove.

AgniLift is a research and development initiative. We separate demonstrated results from open questions, and we don't dress concepts up as products.

  • Safety-led system development
  • Human-controlled operations
  • Compliance with applicable Indian regulations
  • Protection of sensitive technical information
  • Responsible dual-use development
  • No unsupported performance claims
AgniLift does not present conceptual designs as certified products. Specifications and capabilities will be published only after appropriate testing and validation.
