The core thesis

Why bet on combustion when the whole industry is going electric?

🔋

The battery ceiling

Li-ion packs store ~150–250 Wh/kg. Petrol stores ~12,000 Wh/kg (≈3,000 Wh/kg usable after engine losses). For missions where time-aloft × payload matters, chemistry — not motors — is the wall.

🜂

Your unfair advantage

Small-engine, combustion and thermal-systems expertise is rare in a market crowded with electronics and software teams. A better micro-powerplant is a defensible, hardware-deep moat.

🇮🇳

Right market, right time

India is subsidising agri-drones, pushing “Make in India” manufacturing incentives, and modernising defence UAVs — while diesel/petrol logistics are everywhere in rural India. Fuel is the incumbent infrastructure.

Three ways to use a fuel powerplant

ArchitectureHow it worksBest forTrade-off
Series hybrid recommended start Small IC engine spins a generator → charges a buffer battery → electric motors fly the aircraft. Multirotors & VTOL needing 2–4 h endurance with electric-grade control. Two energy conversions; added generator/rectifier weight.
Direct-drive IC Engine drives a propeller directly (like a model plane), electrics only for avionics. Fixed-wing long-range ISR & mapping; simplest, lightest for cruise. Poor hover control; not suited to precise multirotor tasks.
Parallel hybrid Both engine and motor can drive the prop/shaft; motor boosts for takeoff, engine cruises. Heavy-lift VTOL, cargo — peak power on demand. Most mechanically complex; clutch/gearbox integration.

Deep dive on each — plus efficiency maps, heavy-fuel options, and where hydrogen fits — in the Technology & Literature Survey.

Where these drones sell in India

🌾

Agriculture

Spray & broadcast over large farms; longer sorties mean fewer battery swaps per acre. Backed by subsidy schemes.

🛡️

Defence & ISR

Endurance and heavy-fuel logistics fit military needs; strong “indigenous” procurement pull.

📦

Logistics

Middle-mile & rural delivery where range beats last-mile electric.

🗺️

Survey & mapping

Long fixed-wing sorties cover mining, corridors, and land records in one flight.
