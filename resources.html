Glossary

AUW / MTOW
All-Up Weight / Maximum Take-Off Weight — total flying mass; sets DGCA category.
MAUW
Maximum All-Up Weight — the term the Drone Rules use for categorisation.
BVLOS
Beyond Visual Line of Sight — long-range ops needing special permission.
BSFC
Brake-Specific Fuel Consumption (g/kWh) — engine efficiency; lower is better.
Series hybrid
Engine spins a generator that powers electric motors; engine never drives the prop.
Parallel hybrid
Engine and motor can both drive the propeller/shaft.
Range extender
The engine-generator module that recharges/feeds the battery bus in flight.
HFE
Heavy-Fuel Engine — runs diesel/Jet-A; single-fuel logistics, valued by defence.
EFI
Electronic Fuel Injection — improves efficiency & reliability vs carburettor.
Disc loading
Rotor thrust per swept area; lower = more efficient hover.
Specific energy
Energy stored per kg (Wh/kg) — where fuel beats batteries ~10–20×.
VTOL
Vertical Take-Off and Landing.
ISR
Intelligence, Surveillance & Reconnaissance (defence use case).
DaaS
Drone-as-a-Service — selling flight-hours/outcomes, not hardware.
Type Certificate (TC)
DGCA approval that a drone model meets design/safety/airworthiness standards.
UIN
Unique Identification Number — every Indian drone must carry one.
RPTO
Remote Pilot Training Organisation — DGCA-approved pilot certification.
SHG
Self-Help Group — the beneficiary unit under Namo Drone Didi.
FPO
Farmer Producer Organisation — potential agri-drone buyer/channel.

Further reading & benchmarks

  • Hybrid UAV product pages — Harris Aerial (Carrier H6), Skyfront (Perimeter 8), YANGDA, Quaternium HYBRiX — study their published endurance/payload numbers as your performance bar.
  • UAV engine makers — Desert Aircraft / INF Inject (DA-50 EFI), 3W, HFE International — for engine specs & procurement.
  • Flight-control stacks — PX4, ArduPilot documentation — for the avionics you'll integrate.
  • Academic literature — search "hybrid electric propulsion multirotor", "heavy fuel engine UAV", "series hybrid endurance UAV feasibility" (e.g. JSDEWES feasibility studies) for design methodology.
  • Market reports — MarketsandMarkets, IMARC, Ken Research India drone reports (paid); triangulate their headline numbers.
