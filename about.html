About

Building an Indian UAV research company

AgniLift UAV Technologies is an Indian research-led initiative exploring compact engine-powered and hybrid unmanned aerial vehicles.

The initiative began with a practical engineering question: can compact propulsion systems help small and medium UAVs operate longer, carry useful mission payloads and work effectively in demanding field environments?

Our objective is to turn that question into tested engineering, validated prototypes and responsible real-world solutions.

Mission

What we are here to do

To develop dependable UAV propulsion and aircraft technologies that expand the practical possibilities of unmanned flight.

Vision

Where we want to contribute

To contribute to India's UAV ecosystem through original research, responsible engineering and locally developed technical capability.

Values

What guides our work

Engineering integrity

Decisions grounded in physics, evidence and careful analysis.

Safety first

Safety treated as a design requirement, not an afterthought.

Evidence over exaggeration

We report what is demonstrated and clearly mark what is not.

Responsible innovation

Human-controlled, lawful and considerate of wider impact.

Indigenous research

Original, locally developed technical capability.

Practical field impact

Technology shaped by real operating conditions and users.
