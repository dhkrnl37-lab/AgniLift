Research

Research before claims

We would rather publish honest research questions than unproven performance figures. This page outlines how we work, what we are studying, and what remains uncertain.

01 — Mission

Our research mission

To investigate whether compact engine and hybrid power systems can dependably extend the endurance and payload capability of small and medium UAVs — and to do so with engineering rigour, safety and honest reporting.

How we work
01

Define the mission and its real constraints first.

02

Compare propulsion options on the basis of physics and evidence.

03

Test components on the bench before trusting them in the air.

04

Progress through controlled flight testing with data at each step.

05

Separate what is demonstrated from what is still a hypothesis.

02 — Literature overview

Propulsion approaches — a qualitative comparison

Engine-powered and hybrid UAVs are an established but specialised field internationally. The comparison below is qualitative and directional; it avoids specific performance figures, which depend heavily on design and must be established through testing.

Qualitative comparison — not measured results
ConsiderationBattery-electricDirect-drive ICSeries hybrid
Typical enduranceShorterLonger (fixed-wing)Longer
Hover control precisionExcellentNot suited to hoverRetains electric control
Payload headroomLower for long flightsHigherHigher
Mechanical simplicitySimplestSimpleMore complex
Noise & vibrationVery lowHigherModerate
Refuel / rechargeRecharge or swapRefuel quicklyRefuel quickly
Best-fit missionsShort-duration tasksLong-range fixed-wingEndurance multirotor & VTOL
Each approach has genuine strengths. Battery-electric UAVs are well suited to many missions; our research focuses on the subset where endurance and payload are the binding constraints.
03 — Open problems

Technical unknowns we acknowledge

  • Sustained efficiency and reliability of compact engines under continuous flight loads.
  • Energy-management strategies across engine, generator and buffer battery.
  • Vibration isolation that protects sensitive payloads and avionics.
  • Thermal management for continuous-duty operation.
  • Fuel-system safety and field-handling simplicity.
  • Whole-system reliability and maintenance over time.
04 — Planned testing

How we intend to test

Flight

Pilots

Controlled trials with research and industry collaborators.
