Applications

UAV Technology for Demanding Missions

Extended endurance and practical payload capacity could support a range of field operations. The areas below are potential directions for our research.

These are potential application areas under research, not claims of deployed or commercially available capability. Any operation would be subject to testing, validation and applicable regulatory approvals.

Agriculture

Mission challenge

Large fields and repeated passes can require long working time, frequent battery changes and support equipment.

Potential UAV role

Precision spraying, seeding, crop monitoring and transport of agricultural materials.

Why endurance matters

Longer sorties could reduce interruptions, helping operators cover more area within a working window.

Relevant payload examples

Spray tanks and nozzles, spreaders, multispectral and RGB cameras.

Research status

Early research · not deployed

Survey & mapping

Mission challenge

Mapping large or remote areas can require many flights and significant time in the field.

Potential UAV role

Long-duration aerial data collection for land, mining, construction and environmental assessment.

Why endurance matters

Greater time aloft could allow more ground to be covered per flight, improving data continuity.

Relevant payload examples

Mapping cameras, LiDAR, and positioning systems for accurate georeferencing.

Research status

Early research · not deployed

Industrial inspection

Mission challenge

Infrastructure such as power lines, pipelines and solar sites can extend over long, difficult-to-reach corridors.

Potential UAV role

Infrastructure observation, corridor inspection, mapping and operation around remote industrial sites.

Why endurance matters

Longer routes could be inspected in fewer flights, reducing repositioning and downtime.

Relevant payload examples

High-resolution and thermal cameras, zoom optics and sensor gimbals.

Research status

Early research · not deployed

Remote logistics

Mission challenge

Difficult terrain and limited infrastructure can make ground transport slow, costly or unreliable.

Potential UAV role

Transport of equipment, supplies and essential materials across remote or difficult terrain.

Why endurance matters

Range and payload together determine how far useful loads can be carried between stops.

Relevant payload examples

Secured cargo modules and temperature-aware carriers for sensitive items.

Research status

Early research · not deployed

Disaster response

Mission challenge

Emergencies demand rapid awareness and support in areas where infrastructure may be damaged or absent.

Potential UAV role

Situational awareness, emergency supply delivery, communications support and search assistance.

Why endurance matters

Sustained time on station could support continuous observation and coordination during a response.

Relevant payload examples

Cameras, thermal sensors, small supply modules and communications relays.

Research status

Early research · not deployed

Environmental observation

Mission challenge

Monitoring forests, water bodies and land change often spans wide areas over repeated visits.

Potential UAV role

Long-duration observation for conservation, land-use and environmental-assessment research.

Why endurance matters

Extended flights could improve spatial coverage and consistency of collected data.

Relevant payload examples

Multispectral, thermal and RGB imaging, and air- or environment-sampling instruments.

Research status

Early research · not deployed

Defence support

Mission challenge

Support operations can require endurance and reliable logistics in remote or difficult environments.

Potential UAV role

Authorized reconnaissance, logistics, communications relay, border observation and search-and-rescue support.

Why endurance matters

Longer time aloft could support sustained observation and communications in support roles.

Relevant payload examples

Observation sensors, communications relays and supply modules for support tasks.

Scope & status

Support roles only AgniLift focuses on non-weaponized support capabilities — logistics, reconnaissance, communications relay and search-and-rescue — subject to applicable laws and approvals. Early research · not deployed
